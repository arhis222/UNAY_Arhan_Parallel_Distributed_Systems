RELIABLE BROADCAST - SPECIFICATION
Arhan UNAY

Based on L2-Diverses-Techniques, slides 15-18.

1. Purpose
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
A member broadcasts a message to the whole group, including itself.
Receiving means that the library gets the message from a channel. Delivering
means that the library passes it to the application.

2. Requirements
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- All correct members eventually deliver a broadcast message, or none do.
- A correct sender's broadcast eventually reaches all correct members.
- Each member delivers a message at most once.
- Delivered messages must originate from a member's broadcast.
- There is no common delivery-order guarantee between different messages.

Here, correct means that the process does not fail during the execution.
These requirements do not guarantee agreement with deliveries made only by
processes that later fail. That stronger property is called uniform agreement.

3. Assumptions and scope
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
I will use a fixed group whose members know each other. Links between correct
members are FIFO and lossless. Processes are fail-stop: after a crash they
cannot send or receive. A slow process is not automatically considered dead.

The basic protocol tolerates crashes without restarts. A restart would need
message history and fresh incarnation identifiers; resetting a sequence
number and reusing the same sender identity would not be safe.
